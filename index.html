How to play

Every town has to be connected to the grid, and cable is expensive. The plain network joins the towns directly using the least cable possible.

You can do better by adding junctions: extra points where three or more lines meet. Tap the board to add one, drag it to fine-tune, and tap it again to remove it. Your score is how much shorter your network is than the plain one.

Junctions work best where lines meet at about 120°. A hollow junction isn't helping yet: it needs at least three lines.

When you're happy, press Fight the algorithm. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day, and the same opponent.

The algorithm

It's a genetic algorithm. It starts with a population of {POP} random guesses, each a set of junctions. Every generation the best quarter survive and breed: children take some junctions from each parent, then mutate by gaining, losing or nudging a junction. After {GENS} generations the best network wins.

Its nudges start big and get smaller, so it explores first and fine-tunes at the end. Watch the chart after you fight to see it learn.
